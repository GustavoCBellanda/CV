01 — About

About

In four years I went from automating spreadsheets to architecting data and AI platforms for large operations — and building a company around it.

2022 Intern automating processes at an automotive group
2023 — 2024 Computer vision in production on the factory floor
2025 — now Data platforms, enterprise AI and SaaS products, with my own company

Production engineer by degree, software engineer by calling. Since 2022 I've been building software that ships to production: I started automating processes at an automotive group, spent two years putting computer vision on the factory floor, and today I design data platforms, enterprise AI and SaaS products.

Through Bellanda Intelligence, my company, I architect and deliver end-to-end data platforms for corporate clients — from a lakehouse unifying more than two decades of history for a Mitsubishi and Suzuki dealership group, to RevOps systems that connect marketing, sales, after-sales and finance into a single decision logic. In parallel, I lead the technical architecture of SaaS products with partners — including an AI-first operating system for used-vehicle dealers.

I work with a heavily AI-assisted engineering flow: agents with rules and skills versioned per project, planning with a strong model and executing with a fast one, and architecture invariants the model itself respects. To me, AI doesn't replace judgment — it amplifies whoever has the criteria to know what to ask and how to evaluate the result.

My stack is an ecosystem, not a religion: Python (FastAPI, Granian, asyncpg) and TypeScript (React, TanStack) day to day, Rust for the paths where performance truly matters, PostgreSQL, Polars and DuckDB for data. I use whatever tool solves the problem robustly.

I'm 26 and I treat fundamentals as a long-term advantage — systems, low-level and Rust are continuous study, not a fad. A perfectionist about architecture, in love with benchmarks and high performance.
